# Privacy Policy for OfficialX

**Last Updated: May 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **OfficialX** ("the Bot") collects, stores, and uses data when added to your Discord server. By inviting OfficialX to your server, you agree to the terms outlined below.

---

## 1. Data We Collect
To provide security, moderation, and anti-bypass features, OfficialX may temporarily process or store the following information:
* **Discord IDs:** User IDs, Server (Guild) IDs, Channel IDs, and Role IDs.
* **Log Data:** Moderation actions (bans, kicks, mutes), raid triggers, and configuration settings set by server administrators.
* **Content:** Temporary message tracking used strictly for anti-spam and anti-nuke detection.

## 2. How We Use the Data
We use the collected information solely to:
* Maintain server security and execute moderation commands.
* Prevent raiding, nuking, and unauthorized modifications within protected servers.
* Enforce anti-bypass logging features to keep communities safe.

*We do not sell, trade, or share your data with any third-party services.*

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion
* **Automatic Deletion:** All server configuration data and logs are automatically flagged for deletion if the Bot leaves a server.
* **Manual Request:** Server owners and users can request immediate data removal. If you wish to have your data manually deleted from our system, please contact us through our official channels.

## 4. Updates to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in bot functionality or Discord Developer Policies. Continued use of the Bot constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Contact & Support
If you have any questions or requests regarding your data, please reach out to us via our Support Server or open an issue in this repository.
